# DocStation

> DocStation is a unified pharmacy care and medical billing platform that helps community and clinical pharmacies bill insurance for the clinical services they already provide — vaccines, MTM, point-of-care testing, consultations, and DME. It consolidates patient records, medical claims, scheduling, messaging, workflow automation, and revenue analytics into one HIPAA-compliant, SOC 2 Type II system. No public API is documented at this time; this profile is maintained by the API Evangelist network.
